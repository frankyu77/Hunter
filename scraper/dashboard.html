Unlock your data

Your votes and applications are published on this page encrypted. Your passphrase (the HUNTER_PASSPHRASE repo secret) decrypts them here, in this browser. It is never sent anywhere.

Remembering keeps a derived key in this browser that can't be read back out, only used to decrypt. Use it only on your own devices.
